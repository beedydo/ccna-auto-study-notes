🚦 Status codes and headers that decide the answer T50.05

200answered, not "it worked": SD-WAN, UCSM, ACI put failure in the body
201 / 204created (+Location) / done, no body
202accepted: poll the task (Catalyst Center)
401 / 403who are you? (missing, expired) / you may not (role, scope, XSRF)
404 / 409no such path / already exists, lock held
429slow down: wait Retry-After
PagingLink rel=next (Meraki, Webex) · offset+limit (FMC) · OData $top (Intersight)
Trap: Accept = what I want back, Content-Type = what I am sending. ISE without Accept: application/json replies in XML
