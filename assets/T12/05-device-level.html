8 HTTP calls for one job: 3 × GET (read each switch's ports) + 5 × PATCH (shut each DOWN port), vs 2 at controller level.
Trap: using RESTCONF or NETCONF yourself is device level, even though a controller may use the same protocols southbound.
Pros: granular, no controller dependency. Cons: if sw3 fails after sw1 and sw2 succeeded, cleaning up is your job.
