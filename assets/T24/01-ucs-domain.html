One UCSM = one domain. handle.login() → logged in to UCS-SG-DC1: the domain name, not a server. Ten FI pairs = ten UCSMs (until Intersight).
Trap: UCS Manager runs on the fabric interconnects, not on a separate server or VM. Its whole API is one URL: POST /nuova.
Every server's LAN, SAN and management traffic goes through the FIs, so query_classid("computeBlade") sees every blade in the domain.
